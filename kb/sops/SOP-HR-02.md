# SOP-HR-02 Workday sign-in problems

Applies to: Workday HR portal
Symptoms: cannot log in to workday, workday access denied, workday page not loading

Steps for the user:
1. Open Workday only through the single sign-on portal, not through an old bookmark.
2. Clear browser cookies for Workday or use a private browser window.
3. Check that you can sign in to other applications; if not, follow SOP-IAM-02.
4. Try again after 15 minutes if you are a new employee, as accounts are created overnight.

Escalation: Escalate to HR Applications Support if only Workday fails after these steps.
