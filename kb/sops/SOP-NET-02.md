# SOP-NET-02 VPN slow or disconnecting

Applies to: VPN / Remote Access
Symptoms: vpn slow, vpn drops, disconnects every few minutes, unstable connection from home

Steps for the user:
1. Test the home connection without VPN (for example a speed test). If it is slow, restart the home router.
2. Use a wired connection or move closer to the Wi-Fi router.
3. Disconnect and reconnect the VPN, choosing the nearest gateway.
4. Update the VPN client from the Company Portal.
5. Close large uploads or streaming while connected to the VPN.

Escalation: Escalate to Network & Remote Access if the connection drops more than three times an hour on a good home connection.
