# SOP-GEN-01 Major incident handling

Applies to: *
Symptoms: many users affected, store systems down, business-critical service unavailable

Steps for the user:
1. Do not try workarounds that could make the situation worse.
2. Record which service is affected, since when, how many users or sites, and the business impact.
3. Escalate immediately to the resolver group by phone and mark the ticket as a major incident.
4. Send a status update to affected users every 30 minutes.

Escalation: This procedure is always handled by service-desk staff, never automatically.
