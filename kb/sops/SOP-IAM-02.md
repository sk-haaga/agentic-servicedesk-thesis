# SOP-IAM-02 Account locked or new phone for MFA

Applies to: Microsoft Entra ID / single sign-on
Symptoms: account locked, too many attempts, new phone authenticator, mfa not working

Steps for the user:
1. Wait 15 minutes; accounts unlock automatically after repeated failed attempts.
2. Make sure that no old device is trying to sign in with an old password (for example a phone email app).
3. For a new phone, sign in at mysignins.example with your old phone or a temporary access pass and add the new device.
4. If you have no access to any registered method, contact the service desk to verify your identity.

Escalation: Escalate to Identity & Access Management for a temporary access pass or if the account stays locked.
