# SOP-CRM-02 Salesforce access or sign-in problems

Applies to: Salesforce CRM
Symptoms: cannot log in to salesforce, salesforce access denied, insufficient privileges

Steps for the user:
1. Sign in through the single sign-on portal.
2. If the message is 'insufficient privileges', note the record or page you tried to open.
3. Ask your manager to confirm the access you need.
4. Clear browser cookies and try again.

Escalation: Escalate to CRM Support with the manager's approval for a permission change.
