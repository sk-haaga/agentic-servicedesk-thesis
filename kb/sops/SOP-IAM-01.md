# SOP-IAM-01 Reset a forgotten password

Applies to: Microsoft Entra ID / single sign-on
Symptoms: forgot password, password expired, reset password

Steps for the user:
1. Go to the self-service password reset page passwordreset.aurora-retail.example.
2. Verify your identity with the authenticator app or your registered phone.
3. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters that you have not used before.
4. Sign out of all applications and sign in again with the new password; update it on your phone's email app.

Escalation: Escalate to Identity & Access Management if you cannot verify your identity.
