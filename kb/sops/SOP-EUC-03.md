# SOP-EUC-03 Printer problems

Applies to: Windows laptop / endpoint
Symptoms: cannot print, printer offline, print job stuck

Steps for the user:
1. Check that the printer is switched on and has paper and toner.
2. Remove stuck jobs from the print queue and print again.
3. Remove the printer in Settings > Printers and add it again from the Company Portal.
4. Try printing to another printer to see whether the problem is the laptop or the printer.

Escalation: Escalate to End-User Computing if the printer itself shows an error.
