# SOP-TMS-01 Teams audio or video problems in meetings

Applies to: Microsoft Teams
Symptoms: no sound, microphone not working, camera not detected, echo, others cannot hear me

Steps for the user:
1. In Teams, open Settings > Devices and select the correct speaker, microphone and camera.
2. Make a test call from Settings > Devices > Make a test call.
3. Close other applications that may use the camera or microphone.
4. Unplug and reconnect the headset or docking station, then restart Teams.
5. Check the Windows privacy settings: Settings > Privacy > Microphone and Camera must allow Teams.

Escalation: Escalate to Messaging & Collaboration Support if the problem continues after a restart of the laptop.
