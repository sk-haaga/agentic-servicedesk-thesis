# SOP-TMS-02 Teams sign-in loop or Teams will not start

Applies to: Microsoft Teams
Symptoms: teams keeps asking to sign in, blank white screen, teams crashes on start

Steps for the user:
1. Quit Teams completely from the system tray.
2. Clear the Teams cache: delete the contents of %appdata%\Microsoft\Teams (classic) or reset the new Teams app in Settings > Apps.
3. Start Teams and sign in with your work account.
4. If the problem continues, use Teams on the web (teams.microsoft.com) while the desktop app is checked.

Escalation: Escalate to Messaging & Collaboration Support if Teams on the web also fails.
