# SOP-SEC-01 Suspected phishing email

Applies to: *
Symptoms: phishing, suspicious email, clicked a link, fake login page

Steps for the user:
1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply to the email.
2. Report the email with the 'Report phishing' button in Outlook.
3. If you entered your password on a suspicious page, reset it immediately (SOP-IAM-01).
4. Tell the service desk whether you clicked a link or opened an attachment.

Escalation: Always escalate to Security Operations; security incidents are never resolved automatically.
